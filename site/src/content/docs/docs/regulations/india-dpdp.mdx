## Applicability

Deployment, offering, data subjects or data residency in India. Children's data amplifies.

## Risk tiers and obligations

| Tier | Label | Obligations attached |
| --- | --- | --- |
| high | Significant data fiduciary workload | Data Protection Impact Assessment; Data Protection Officer in India; independent data audit |
| limited | Standard data fiduciary workload | consent notice; grievance redressal mechanism |
| minimal | Minimal risk | none |

High tier: credit scoring, employment screening, medical diagnosis. Systems affecting
minors escalate to *high*.
